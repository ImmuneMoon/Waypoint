PRIVACY POLICY
==================================================

1. Introduction
Nucleus Games and Fulllion Creative Works ("us", "we", or "our") operate the Waypoint desktop application. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

2. Information Collection, Use, and Local Storage
Waypoint is designed primarily as a local desktop application.
- Local Data (Campaigns and Maps): The data you enter into Waypoint (including campaign plans, nodes, and map data) is stored locally on your device in the App's installation directory (specifically the "saves" folder). We do not collect, transmit, or store this local data on our servers. You have full control over this data.
- Multiplayer Profile: If you use multiplayer, the display name you choose is stored locally on your device alongside a randomly generated identifier. No account is created, and neither is sent to us.
- Contact Information: If you contact us via email (fulllioncreativeworks@gmail.com), we will use your email address and any information you provide solely to respond to your inquiries.
- Usage Data: As a standalone desktop application, we do not inherently collect telemetry or usage analytics.

3. Multiplayer Data Transmission
Multiplayer sessions are peer-to-peer: data travels directly between the participants' devices, not through servers we operate.
- What is shared: When you host a session, the campaign content the App presents to players (maps, whiteboards, images, token positions) and any chat messages are transmitted directly to the devices of users who join with your room code. GM-only material (planner documents, room notes, character dossiers, hidden items, and GM note cards) is filtered out before transmission and is not sent to players.
- Connection brokering: To establish the peer-to-peer connection, the App uses the public PeerJS signaling service. That service handles connection setup metadata (such as session identifiers and IP addresses, as is inherent to establishing any direct connection) but does not receive your campaign content. Participants in a session may be able to observe each other's IP addresses, as with any peer-to-peer technology.
- Relay fallback: When two networks cannot establish a direct connection, session traffic may be carried through a public TURN relay service (Open Relay). Relayed traffic remains encrypted in transit (DTLS); the relay operator cannot read your campaign content, and we still receive nothing.
- No retention by us: We do not receive, store, or have access to anything transmitted in a session. Content received from a session exists only in the participant's running App and is not written to their disk by the App.

4. Data Deletion
Since your campaign and save data is stored locally on your machine, you can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the application and choosing the option to wipe local save data, or by manually deleting the "saves" folder within the installation directory. Your multiplayer profile can be cleared by clearing the App's local storage (the "system/userdata" folder).

5. Disclosure of Data
Nucleus Games and Fulllion Creative Works will not sell or rent any Personal Data. We may disclose personal information you provide via email if required to comply with a legal obligation, protect our rights, or protect against legal liability.

6. Security of Data
The security of your locally stored campaign data is dependent on the security of your own device. Multiplayer traffic is carried over WebRTC data channels, which are encrypted in transit (DTLS) by design. We take reasonable precautions to protect any information you transmit to us via email.
